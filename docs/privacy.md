# Privacy & Data Sovereignty

OpenSand is designed to run entirely on your local hardware:

- **Local Storage:** All sessions, transcripts, agent rules, and memories are saved in `~/.opensand/` on your Mac.
- **Zero Telemetry:** OpenSand does not collect telemetry, analytics, or behavioral tracking.
- **No Remote Telemetry Server:** No pingbacks or cloud phone-home endpoints exist in the codebase.
- **Local Inference:** When paired with Ollama, no prompt tokens, file contents, or conversation text ever leave your computer.
